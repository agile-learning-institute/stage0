# Exercise Template: Observe/Reflect/Make Structure

Use this template for creating new design chat exercises that follow the Observe/Reflect/Make framework.

## [Exercise Title]
**Purpose**: [Brief statement of what this exercise aims to accomplish]

### Observe
**Instructions**: [How to gather individual observations]
- Keep observations brief - think of what you could write on a 3"x3" sticky with a dull sharpie
- Practice "yes and" thinking, writing, and speaking - add your ideas, don't tear others down
- Give all your ideas, include magical or absurd thinking
- We are Observing as individuals, no discussion or cross-talk during the Observe phase
- Quantity over quality, better 10 crazy ideas than 1 "Best" idea

**Format**: [Specify the expected format for observations]
- Examples: List format (one item per reply), categorical observation, structured format, ranking lists, etc.

**Completion Signal**: [How to indicate Observe phase is complete]
- React with 👍 when you have completed your observations
- Facilitator will call transition when sufficient observations gathered

### Reflect
**Instructions**: [How to synthesize observations into shared understanding]
- Replace placeholder text with synthesized observations
- Use formatting to indicate patterns, consensus, or groupings
- Build upon previous contributions to create coherent summary

**Format**: [Specify the expected format for reflection]
- Examples: Bulleted lists with consensus formatting, grouped categories with headings, patterned outlines, etc.

**Completion Signal**: [How to indicate Reflect phase is complete]
- When the synthesized reflection is complete and understood by all
- Facilitator verifies shared understanding before proceeding

### Make
**Instructions**: [How to convert understanding into decisions/actions]
- Propose and agree on concrete outcomes
- Create actionable items: decisions, assignments, schedules, plans
- Document commitments within the exercise post
- Often involves scheduling follow-up exercises or making assignments

**Format**: [Specify the expected format for making decisions/actions]
- Examples: Lists of unique ideas with names, roadmaps (now/next/later), action item assignments, etc.

**Completion Signal**: [How to indicate Make phase is complete]
- When concrete outcomes are documented and agreed upon
- Clear next steps are established
